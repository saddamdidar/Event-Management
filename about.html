Our Story

Built one celebration at a time

Elena Marquez spent eight years as a florist for hotel ballrooms before starting Festoria in 2014. She had one hatchback full of balloons, a hand pump and a simple idea: design every event around the people in it, arrive early, and leave the room more beautiful than anyone imagined.

The first jobs were birthday garlands in Grant Park and Decatur, booked by word of mouth. Clients kept coming back for the next birthday, the baby shower and then the wedding, so the studio grew one referral at a time. Today Festoria has a design studio in Old Fourth Ward, a rental warehouse and a team of 24 stylists, florists and installers. We still do what we did on day one, just for a lot more celebrations.
